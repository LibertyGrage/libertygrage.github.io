Roadster X controls

These are for the car's own systems. Driving itself (accelerate, brake, steer, horn, camera) stays the game's. Controller buttons use Xbox names. The game shows the controls for the moment on screen when you get in.

Hops, ramp jumps and ledge drops are caught near the ground automatically. In a jump or a drop, RT (W) only spins the wheels up, so you drive off fast when you land; in the hover it climbs.

An option in plugins\RoadsterX.ini, [Gas] Infinite=1, gives an air reserve that never runs out.

Leaving the car

Use the game's exit button (F, or Y on a controller). From that moment the car's systems take no input, so nothing launches while you climb out.

Changing the controls

Every key and button can be changed in plugins\RoadsterX.ini ([Keys], [Pad] and [Controls]). Each line has a comment. To apply a change without restarting, edit [Live] Serial (for example 0 to 1) and save.
