AI use

Some mod sites require this disclosure.

Tandem T6 was made with AI coding assistants (Claude by Anthropic and OpenAI Codex), directed by the creator.

  • Written by the AI assistants: the plugin code, the Blender scripts that generate the model, the tools that convert it to GTA IV's formats, the code that generates the textures and the sounds, and these documents.
  • Done by the creator: the idea, the look and the features, every decision along the way, and testing the car in game.
  • No AI image, video or audio generators were used. The model is generated from code, the textures and the 20 sounds are synthesized by code, and the pictures on this page are renders of the mod's own model.
