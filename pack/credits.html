Credits

  • Author: Penandpaerdamn
  • Built with:
    • MinGW-w64 (GCC) to build the plugins, and clang for their tests
    • Python 3 for the build, release and test tools
  • Third-party code and assets: none are included. The plugins, their settings files and the two cars are our own. No Rockstar files, textures or sounds are included, and nothing extracted from the game is shipped: the episode weapons are built on your PC from your own copy of the game.

Standing on the shoulders of

The pack only works because of these community projects. None of them are included: you download them from their authors' pages (see docs/REQUIREMENTS.md). Thank you.

  • FusionFix by ThirteenAG and the FusionFix team, which also brings the ASI loader
  • Aru, for the C++ ScriptHook, and the authors of the Complete Edition hook fix
  • The authors of Liberty's Legacy, the trainer the pack's menus live in
  • The authors of Liberty Loadout, the weapon wheel the pack builds on
  • The GTA IV modding community, whose research into the game's files and script functions made this possible

Recommended alongside the pack, and thanks to their authors too (not included either): Various Fixes, Console Visuals, Higher Resolution Vehicle Pack, Higher Resolution Miscellaneous Pack and Radio Restoration.

Terms of use

Liberty City Pack is free. These terms cover our own files in the release zip (the plugins, their settings files and our text), and the text and images on its Liberty Garage wiki pages. The plugins' source code is not part of either and is not licensed here.

The community mods the pack needs (FusionFix, ScriptHook, the trainer and the others in docs/REQUIREMENTS.md) are not part of the pack. You download them from their authors, and their own terms apply to them.

You may

  • Download it, install it on your own PCs, and play it, free.
  • Show it in screenshots, videos and streams. A link back to the official page is appreciated. Rockstar Games' own video policy covers the game footage itself.
  • Change the settings files for your own use.
  • Put it in a modpack for your own private use.

Please don't

  • Re-upload or mirror it on another site, or in a public modpack. Link to the official page instead.
  • Share edited versions (changed plugins or files) without asking first.
  • Sell it, charge for it, or put it behind a paywall, a subscription or an early-access tier.
  • Take our plugins, art or sounds for another game, mod or product without permission.
  • Claim it as your own work or remove the credits.
