[1.0.0] - 2026-10-18

First public release.

Added

  • Roadster X, an add-on car (roadsterx) for GTA IV: The Complete Edition 1.2.0.59. No stock car is replaced.
  • Two-seat cabin with butterfly doors: the driver and one passenger.
  • Jump: tap to hop over traffic; hold to keep rising, so the hold sets the height, then let go to drop onto the soft landing.
  • Hover and fly on ten cold-gas thrusters: double-tap to hover, then climb (jump button, accelerator, or look up to climb straight up), descend, travel and boost. Double-tap again to drop; the thrusters catch the car just above the ground. In the air, one press of the jump button fires the jets again.
  • Air tricks (flips, rolls, spins), kickflips on the road, and quick 180 and 360 spins.
  • Soft landings for jumps, ramp jumps and ledge drops.
  • Turn smoothly on the spot from a standstill (push further to turn faster), and powered drifts when moving.
  • Active rear wing (Auto, Downforce, Airbrake, Stowed) and speed-scaled downforce with fan suction.
  • Water rescue: the thrusters lift the car back out of water (can be turned off).
  • On-screen systems panel with gauges and the controls that matter right now.
  • Original synthesized sounds: thrusters, wing servo, doors and motor.
  • Every setting in plugins/RoadsterX.ini, reloadable while you play.

Known issues

  • One passenger only (it's a two-seater), and companions can take a moment at the butterfly doors.
  • Developed and tested on a Mac with CrossOver. Windows should behave the same; please report anything that doesn't.
