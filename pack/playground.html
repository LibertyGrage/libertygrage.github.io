The playground

The pack's second half makes free roam more fun to live in. Every feature has an on/off switch in the trainer's menu or the pack's settings file, and the story missions keep working as Rockstar made them: the features that change the city's behaviour step back during missions.

Where things are: on a controller, RB + X opens the trainer (F11 on the keyboard), and the pack's pages are in its menu. Quick Settings is the menu's first row, or hold Select. Start twice opens the garage. LB is the weapon wheel.

Chauffeur

Set a waypoint on the map and let a driver take you there in your own car, or call one with a car from your garage. Pick Calm, Normal or Fast. With no waypoint the driver just cruises. One button stops the ride safely and gives you the wheel back. The camera stays yours the whole time, so it doubles as a sightseeing tour.

Smart traffic and jam relief

In free roam, a big street fight used to freeze whole blocks: every driver waited forever. Now drivers who are stuck for a while go around, panic onto the pavement, or get out and run. Gang members and criminals may take an abandoned car, or a driver's, and some of them end up wanted by the police. While a fight lasts, traffic is routed around its block.

Off by default for now. Jam relief caused short stutters while driving, so it is switched off until its work is spread out better. To try it anyway, set JamReliefAtLaunch=1 under [Driving] in plugins/LibertyCityCore.ini.

Gang life

Liberty City's gangs have rivals and allies, and in free roam they settle it on the streets. Choose the default rivalries, all-out war, or off.

Police and arrests

Optional, off by default. Officers answer gang gunfire, order suspects to drop their weapons, cuff them, walk them to a car and book them, all on their own. You can help: disarm a suspect, order them down, or cuff them yourself. Gangs that keep killing earn their own wanted stars, up to a NOOSE response.

Crew and clown car

Liberty's Legacy already gives you bodyguards. The pack gives them loadouts, makes them get into your car with you, and adds clown car mode: any car takes the whole crew, the way a clown car would, and everyone comes out again when you stop.

Quick settings

The first row of the trainer's menu opens a short page with the settings people change most: seat belt, can't-die mode, crew boarding and the clown car. Can't-die mode isn't invincibility: you still get knocked down and hurt, you just don't die, and falls don't hurt.

HUD and reticle

  • A compact HUD card next to the radar: clock, date, speed, and health and armour bars, replacing the trainer's plain speed text.
  • The game's own aiming reticle takes the colour of your target: red for an enemy (anyone hurt by or shooting at you), orange for a gang member, with the gang's name, blue for police, green for your crew and white for a civilian. Red always wins, except for your crew.

Map

  • Live threat blips in free roam: enemies, your crew, police, armed people and gang members, with no two blips on top of each other or on the game's own icons.
  • Pigeons: the 200 hidden pigeons show as small dots on the radar, and each one you shoot drops off.
  • Locked islands: the crossings to islands you haven't unlocked yet are marked.

Weapons

The Liberty Loadout wheel stays on its button: hold it for the wheel, tap it to holster or draw your weapon.

The episodes in Niko's city

The Complete Edition includes The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony, but their weapons stay in their own games. The pack brings them into Niko's free roam:

  • Episode weapons with their real sounds, their explosives, and the parachute.
  • What it doesn't do: you play as Niko; Johnny's and Luis's stories stay where they are, in the Complete Edition's episode menu.

The pack can't ship Rockstar's files, so on first start it builds this from the episodes in your own copy of the game, and leaves the game's own files alone.

The first start builds this and asks for one restart; the install guide has the details.

Garage, modes and presets

Keep a garage of favourite cars, with their handling, and call one up from the menu. Our own two cars, Tandem T6 and Roadster X, are in it when you install them from the pack.

Switch modes from the menu:

  • Career: the normal game, with everything above.
  • Quiet Drive: an empty city to just drive: no pedestrians, relaxed handling and every island open.
  • Kids Drive: no pedestrians, no weapons, and protected cars.

Presets set how cars feel and how busy the streets are:

  • Handling: Balanced Road, Chill - Normal Handling, Chaos 1 - Heavy and Fast, Chaos 2 - Maximum. Built from your own game's handling, never replacing its files.
  • Traffic: Light, Normal, Busy or Off.

The menu remembers your mode and presets; a fresh install starts in Career.
