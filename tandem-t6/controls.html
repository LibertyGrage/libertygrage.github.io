Tandem T6 controls

The car drives with the game's normal controls. These are the extras. A short hint shows them each time you get in.

ActionKeyboardController (Xbox layout)
Stats: card, live stats, off (cycles)Tap KHold LB, click R3
Reset the timers and the top speedHold K (0.6 s)Hold LB + R3 (0.6 s)
Get in as the driverThe game's Enter vehicle key (F by default)Y
Ride in the back of a Tandem T6 driven by a friend or a companionHold Enter vehicle (F) next to itHold Y next to it
Get out (either seat)The game's exit key (F by default)Y

How the seats work

  • The driver sits in front and the passenger sits directly behind. Both get in through the front doors.
  • The rear passenger opens a front door and climbs through to the back, even when the driver is already in. They get out the same way.
  • Companions (bodyguards, friends) use the rear seat. They walk to a front door by themselves.

The stats

  • Card (shown for a few seconds when you get in): your best 0-60 mph, 0-100 km/h and quarter-mile times this session, the quarter-mile trap speed and your top speed, all measured in game.
  • Live stats: power in kW (and amps), speed in mph and km/h, motor rpm, then the timers, then body roll, cornering g and what the stabiliser is doing.
  • Timers start by themselves: come to a full stop, wait a moment, then accelerate. Hold K (or LB + R3) to clear them.

Notes

  • The keys only work while the game window has focus, and they are ignored while a trainer menu (such as Liberty's Legacy) is open, so menu buttons don't reach the car.
  • L3 stays the game's horn: the car never reads it.

Changing the keys

Open plugins\TandemT6.ini in a text editor:

  • [Keys] Hud=0x4B is the keyboard key as a Windows virtual-key code: 0x4B is K. For example, 0x4C is L and 0x4A is J.
  • [Pad] ChordA=0x0100 and ChordB=0x0080 are the controller buttons as XInput values: 0x0100 is LB and 0x0080 is R3. Others: 0x0200 RB, 0x0040 L3, 0x1000 A, 0x2000 B.
  • [Pad] HoldMs=600 is how long a press counts as a hold, in milliseconds.

To apply a change while the game runs, change [Live] Serial (for example from 0 to 1) and save. The car reloads its settings within a second.
