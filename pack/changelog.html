Changelog

Versions follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, with -beta.N for public test builds and -rc.N for release candidates. Players see them as "0.1 beta 1", "1.0 RC 2" and so on. What the numbers mean. Every release is for GTA IV: The Complete Edition 1.2.0.59 only.

Each version starts with what it is built for, what it needs and what it includes, then lists its changes under Added, Changed, Fixed and Removed, with credit to whoever reported or made each one.

Unreleased

Changed

  • Jam relief (smart traffic around street fights) is now off by default: it caused short stutters while driving. Turn it on with JamReliefAtLaunch=1 under [Driving] in plugins/LibertyCityCore.ini.

[0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-10-18

The first test build for Windows. It has not been run on a Windows PC yet: please read Beta testing first, and report what you find with the bug report form.

  • Built for: GTA IV: The Complete Edition 1.2.0.59 (Steam or Rockstar Games Launcher) on Windows 10 or 11.
  • Needs: FusionFix 5.1.2, ScriptHook 0.5.1 with the Complete Edition hook fix, Liberty's Legacy 2.4.1 and Liberty Loadout 0.18.0 (set to the LB button, see the install guide). Links in REQUIREMENTS.
  • Includes: LibertyCityCore, CompactHUD, LBInteractionCompat, PedSpawnGuard and LibertyEpisodes; our car Tandem T6 1.0.0-rc.5 as an optional folder.
  • Saves: back up your saves before installing, as the install guide says.

Added

  • Liberty City menus inside the trainer: Career, Quiet Drive and Kids Drive, traffic and handling presets, switched live and kept for the next start. Handling presets are applied to your own game's handling data.
  • The garage, the chauffeur, smart traffic and jam relief, bodyguards and crew, police and arrests, and guards against known game crashes (LibertyCityCore).
  • The HUD card, target-coloured reticle, map blips and the world-loading fix (CompactHUD).
  • The LB button: tap to holster, hold for the Liberty Loadout wheel (LBInteractionCompat).
  • A bodyguard spawn guard for Liberty's Legacy (PedSpawnGuard).
  • The episodes in Niko's city: the guns of The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony with their own sounds and explosions, and the parachute, playing as Niko (LibertyEpisodes). Built on the first start from your own game files, then restart the game once; no game files are shipped.
  • Tandem T6 as an optional folder of plain files; its data lines are added for you on the first start.

Built on FusionFix, ScriptHook, Liberty's Legacy and Liberty Loadout by their authors (see CREDITS).
