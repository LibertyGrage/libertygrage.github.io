AI use

Some mod sites require this disclosure.

Roadster X was made with AI coding assistants (Anthropic's Claude, through Claude Code, and OpenAI Codex). The assistants wrote most of the code under Penandpaerdamn's direction:

  • the plugin (thrusters, flight, wing, panel, controls);
  • the Blender model script;
  • the file-format tools and the sound-synthesis scripts;
  • these documents.

Penandpaerdamn designed the car and its controls, directed every change, chose what shipped, and tested it in the game.

The model and its textures come from the build scripts, and the sounds from the synthesis scripts. The download contains no AI-generated images, voices or music.
